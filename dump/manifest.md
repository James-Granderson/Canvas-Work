Instructions on how to use a budgeting spreadsheet are provided below. 📊 How to Use a Budgeting Spreadsheet 1. Clear out any sample numerical values on your Spreadsheet 2. Start with Your Income Locate the “Monthly Income” section on the second tab of the spreadsheet. Enter all your sources of income (e.g., part-time job, financial aid, family support). The spreadsheet may automatically total your income—if not, add a formula like =SUM(B2:B5) in the total cell. 3. List Your Fixed Expenses Locate the “Monthly Expenses” section on the third tab of the spreadsheet. First, enter your “Fixed Expenses” section with their corresponding amounts. Next, enter your “ Variable Expenses” section with estimated or actual amounts. 4. Track Your Savings Use the “Savings” section to list money you're setting aside (e.g., emergency fund, savings account, vacation fund). 5. Compare Income and Expenses The spreadsheet may show a “Remaining Balance” or “Net Income” field. Formula: Total Income - (Fixed + Variable Expenses + Savings) A positive number means you're within budget. A negative number means you’re overspending and may need to adjust. 6. Update Weekly or Monthly Update your actual spending weekly or monthly to stay on track. Compare “Planned” vs. “Actual” to see where you can improve. 7. Use Charts and Graphs (Optional) Some spreadsheets include visualizations to show spending trends. Use pie charts or bar graphs to get a quick overview of where your money is going. Happy Budgeting!!!
